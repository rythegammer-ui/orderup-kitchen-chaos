Two scheduled visits timed to Southwest Florida's seasons, so you never think about your gutters again.

Visit 1: Late May

What clogs gutters in Fort Myers

It's not fall leaves like up north. Here it's live oak leaves and tassels in March and April, pine needles year-round, palm fronds and seed pods, roofing granules from shingle roofs, and wasp and bird nests in the downspouts. Mix it with daily rain and you get a black sludge that holds water, breeds mosquitoes and rusts the gutter from the inside.

If you have screened-in gutters or guards, we clean the guards too. Guards reduce debris; they don't eliminate it.
