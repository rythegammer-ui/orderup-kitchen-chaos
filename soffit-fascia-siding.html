Fascia: the board your gutters hang on

Fascia is the board along the roof edge that gutters attach to. In Fort Myers it takes constant abuse: overflowing gutters soak it, humidity keeps it damp, and the sun cooks it. Once wood fascia starts to rot, hangers pull loose, gutters sag and the problem feeds itself.

We replace rotted sections with new primed wood or install aluminum fascia wrap that never needs paint, and we do it before hanging new gutters. If you've been quoted new gutters by someone who didn't look at your fascia, get a second opinion.

Soffit: ventilation, pests and wind

Soffit is the panel under the eave. Vented aluminum or vinyl soffit lets hot attic air escape, which matters in a state where attics hit 140 degrees. Loose or missing soffit panels are also the main way rats, squirrels and wasps get into Fort Myers attics.

Hurricane winds pry at soffit from below. Since Ian, a lot of homes in Lee County still have panels that were pushed up, rattling or gone entirely. We refasten what's salvageable and replace the rest with matching panels and proper F-channel and J-channel so it stays put.

Siding repair and replacement

Vinyl siding and fiber-cement board (Hardie) are both common here. We patch storm-damaged sections, replace warped or cracked panels, and re-side gable ends and additions. We match existing profiles and colors as closely as the product lines allow, and we'll tell you honestly when a faded wall will need a full side repainted to blend.
