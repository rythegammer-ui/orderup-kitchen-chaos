Why SWFL screens fail

UV is the enemy. Fiberglass screen gets brittle after seven to ten years of Florida sun, and then every storm takes another panel. Roof panels go first because they get the most sun and hold the most water and debris. If you're replacing panels every summer, a full rescreen is usually cheaper than another three years of patching.

We use quality fiberglass or polyester screen and new spline sized to your cage's channel. Structural repairs to the aluminum frame, cables and anchors can be quoted after a site visit.
